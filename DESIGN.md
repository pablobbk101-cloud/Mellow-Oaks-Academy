# Mellow Oaks Academy: design system

Stack: static HTML generated by `tools/build.py` (Cloudflare Workers assets, no build step), so GSAP, ScrollTrigger, SplitText and Lenis are vendored in `vendor/`. Reason: the repo deploys with no build command; adding a framework would break that.

## Concept card
- **Idea:** a school grows like an oak: one ring per phase, from 18 months to Grade 12.
- **Anchor:** the school is named for an oak, its logo is an oak, and it has exactly five phases.
- **Metaphor:** the cross-section of a trunk (tree rings).
- **Signature:** on the home page a pinned trunk section draws one ring per phase as you scroll; the photo in its centre and the text beside it change with each ring. One driver (scroll progress) feeds the rings, the photo, the text and the link.
- **Emotion:** calm, rooted, unhurried.
- **Motion verb:** *grow outward*. Things open from a centre: discs, circle clip-paths, a fill that spreads from the pointer, a page change that opens as a circle from the link you clicked.
- **Screenshot:** the dark hero, a circular photo of the oak-lined path inside thin concentric rings.
- **Idle:** the rings breathe by about 1.5% over seven seconds on the GSAP ticker. Off under reduced motion.

## Type
- Display: Young Serif (Google), single weight, never faux-bolded, no italics.
- Text and UI: Figtree.
- Fluid scale: h1 clamp(2.8rem, 6.4vw, 6rem), h2 clamp(2rem, 3.8vw, 3.4rem), body 17-18px / 1.6.

## Shape and grid
- Circles are the shape. Photos on inner pages sit in discs; cards use a 22px radius.
- 12-column feel, 1180px wrap, 1560px on wide screens.

## Motion language
- One ease: `power3.out` for things arriving, `power2.inOut` for scrubbed work.
- Primary targets (buttons, project and phase links) answer in the verb: a fill grows outward from the pointer.
- Text headings reveal by line mask. Everything else arrives with a small rise.
- Reduced motion: no Lenis, no pin, no reveals, rings fully drawn, all content visible.

## Assumptions
- Content is the school's own (from mellowoaksacademy.co.za); nothing invented.
- The loader is skipped: the site is a school brochure, not a launch.

Grade 3’s 3D Recycled Art Exhibition – Insects & Creepy Crawlies

At Mellow Oaks Academy, creativity is buzzing — quite literally! Our Grade 3 learners recently unveiled their imaginative 3D art, where the theme was insects and creepy crawlies. Using only recycled materials, the young artists transformed everyday waste into extraordinary creatures that crawled, fluttered, and wriggled across the display.

From Trash to Treasure

Plastic bottles became beetle bodies, egg cartons turned into caterpillars, and tin foil was shaped into shimmering dragonfly wings. Each piece showcased how discarded items can be reimagined into something beautiful and meaningful. The learners proved that art doesn’t need expensive supplies — just vision, teamwork, and a commitment to sustainability.

Lessons in Sustainability

Beyond the fun of creating, the project taught learners valuable lessons about caring for the environment. By working with recycled materials, they discovered how waste can be repurposed, reducing our impact on the planet. It was a hands-on way to connect art, science, and sustainability.

Celebrating Young Creativity

The Grade 3 exhibition was a joyful celebration of imagination and eco-consciousness. At Mellow Oaks Academy, we are proud of our learners for showing that even the smallest creatures — and the smallest actions — can make a big difference.
